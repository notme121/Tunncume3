# 📅 How to Add the GI Course 2026 Schedule to Apple Calendar / iCloud

We generated 3 calendar (`.ics`) options to fit your workflow:

### 1. `GI_Course_2026_Schedule.ics` (Recommended)
- **What it is**: Complete GI System Course 2026 schedule (lectures, labs, case discussions, OR/IR rotations, reviews, and exams).
- **How to add to Apple Calendar / iCloud**:
  - **On Mac**: Simply double-click `GI_Course_2026_Schedule.ics`. Calendar will prompt you to choose a calendar or create a new one named **"GI System Course 2026"**. If you select your iCloud account, it will instantly sync to your iPhone and iPad!
  - **In Apple Calendar App**: Go to **File → Import...** → select `GI_Course_2026_Schedule.ics`.
- **Pre-configured Reminders**: Includes alarms 15–30 minutes before classes and 1 day before exams and field trips.

### 2. `CUMEDi_30_Calendar_Updated_With_GI.ics` (Full Semester Merge)
- **What it is**: Merges all 193 existing events from your published `CUMEDi '30 Calendar` (`webcal://p181-caldav.icloud.com/...`) with the entire new GI Module schedule (Oct 19 – Dec 19, 2026).
- **Use case**: If you want your full academic year unified into one calendar file.

### 3. `GI_Course_2026_Mandatory_Only.ics`
- **What it is**: Only the 29 mandatory (`🙋 ✓AC`) sessions, practical classes, clinical observation visits, and exams.
- **Use case**: For a streamlined, distraction-free attendance calendar.
