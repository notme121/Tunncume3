**Behavioral objectives**	  
At the end of the session, students should be able to 

* Explain the mechanism of nausea, vomiting and dysphagia by integration between topics of physiology, anatomy, and biochemistry.   
* Formulate basic differential diagnoses based on clinical history, physical findings, and supportive studies and compare and contrast differing etiologies with similar presentations  
* Discuss the underlying physiologic, biochemical, anatomic, and pathologic factors producing the clinical scenarios in sample cases.  
* Briefly describe pharmacologic and other therapeutic options for the final diagnosis in each sample case.

**Content**  
1\.    Approach to nausea and vomiting  
2\.    Approach to dysphagia  
3\.    Nausea and vomiting from pregnancy, drug, toxin, metabolic disorders   
4\. 	Nausea and vomiting from gastrointestinal tract disorder
