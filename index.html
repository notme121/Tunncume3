Generative AI Policy (CU-MEDi GI Module)

Clear boundaries for ethical, professional medical education

For Learning & Study

Exercise extreme caution: information generated by AI may hallucinate or contain inaccuracies. AI-generated answers must NOT be used as medical references or authoritative citations.

For Clinical Notes & Documentation

Developing proficiency in systematic diagnostic reasoning and independent documentation of patient notes is a non-negotiable core outcome. Do not rely on AI as your primary writer. Allowed use is strictly limited to language validation.

Attendance, Tardiness & Absence Remediation

Zero-tolerance for proxy sign-in; strict remediation workflow

1. Mandatory Sessions (🙋 ✓AC): 100% attendance required. Arriving more than 15 minutes late is automatically recorded as an absence.
2. Location-Based Check-in: Attendance is taken on-site at the actual venue (Pattayapat, Aor Por Ror, CHAMPS, Bhumisiri). Scanning fingerprints at Smart Classroom 1 entrance is not counted for external sessions!
3. Remediation Submission: Absences result in an initial score of “Poor (1)”. To remediate:
  • Submit official Leave Form (Personal business leave requires 3 working days prior notice).
  • Complete assigned remedial make-up work.
  • Email remedial work to cu-medi-gi@chula.md by Friday 5:00 PM of that same week.
🏥

Hospital, Clinical Rotations & OR Conduct

Professional integrity, patient confidentiality and infection control

🚫 Strict Photography & Video Ban

Video recording and photography are strictly prohibited in all clinical care areas, endoscopy suites, procedure rooms, and operating theatres without exception.

🤐 Elevator & Public Space Etiquette

All social discussion or patient case conversations in hospital elevators and public corridors must be strictly avoided to protect patient privacy.

🥼 White Coat & ID Badge

Must be clean, pressed, fully buttoned, with official CU-MEDi name badge visible on chest pocket/collar during all patient encounters (real or simulated).

😷 OR Visit Special Rule (Fri 6 Nov)

Hospital scrubs required. Do NOT bring personal belongings or bags to the OR suite as no storage lockers are provided.
