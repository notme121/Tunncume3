Esophagus Exam Checklist (MHS 234 / MH 109)
1. Mucosa (Mucous Membrane)

  • Muscularis Mucosae: smooth muscle
2. Submucosa (Dense Irregular CT)
  • Mucus Glands: simple columnar epithelium (pale foamy acini)
  • Ducts: simple cuboidal, simple columnar & stratified cuboidal epithelia
3. Muscularis Externa (Mostly Smooth Muscle = Final 1/3)
  • Inner Layer: circular layer of muscle cells
  • Outer Layer: longitudinal layer of muscle cells
4. Adventitia
  • Loose connective tissue outer coat
