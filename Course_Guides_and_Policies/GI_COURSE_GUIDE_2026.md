### In-Class Evaluation Rubric & S/U Grading Rules:

- **Scale 4**: Excellent
- **Scale 3**: Average
- **Scale 2**: Below Average
- **Scale 1**: Poor (including unexcused absence)

> [!WARNING]
> **An Unsatisfactory (U) Grade will be assigned and recorded in the student's Professionalism Record if**:
> 1. Attendance is **less than 100% in mandatory sessions** without approved remediation.
> 2. Make-up work is submitted after the deadline (**Friday 5:00 PM of the same week**) or is poor quality.
> 3. The student receives **“Below Average (2)” in 3 or more** in-class assessments, or **“Poor (1)” in 2 or more**.
> 4. A serious professionalism violation occurs (e.g. proxy attendance scanning, falsification, unexcused absence from formative exam).

## 5. Course Policies & Protocols

### A. Attendance & Absence Remediation Protocol
1. **100% Attendance Rule**: All mandatory (`🙋 ✓AC`) sessions require presence throughout. Arriving >15 minutes late is treated as an absence.
2. **Site-Specific Verification**: Fingerprint check at Smart Classroom 1 entrance is **invalid** for activities located elsewhere (Pattayapat, Aor Por Ror, CHAMPS, Bhumisiri). Attendance is recorded at the physical location.
