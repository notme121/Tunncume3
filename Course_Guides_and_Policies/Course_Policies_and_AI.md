### 2. Generative AI Usage Rules
- **For Learning & Studying**: AI models hallucinate medical facts. **AI output must never be used or cited as an authoritative reference**.
- **For Clinical Documentation & Patient Notes**: Independent clinical reasoning and documentation of progress notes is a non-negotiable competency. **Do not use AI as your primary generator**. Allowed AI use is strictly restricted to language validation.
- Full policy: [CU-MEDi Policy Statement on Generative AI](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_g9kcih1Q-3CVdZWkDY_5oFdZ8rUDCO9fEKurTKHJEs/edit?usp=sharing)
